   fixture plus `newField: true` → expects `valid: true` (tolerant reader

   missing-field error` — `schemaVersion: '2.0'` vs vendored `'1.0'` →
   expects `valid: false`, an error at `/schemaVersion` whose `keyword` is
   **not** `'required'`, and confirms the missing-`score` case (test 2) has
   no error at `/schemaVersion` — proving the two failure causes are
   distinguishable.
