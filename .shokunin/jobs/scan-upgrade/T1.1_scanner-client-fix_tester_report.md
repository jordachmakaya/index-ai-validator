# Report — Tester (fix) — T1.1_scanner-client

## What changed

Exactly the 2 named hanging tests in
`packages/validator/src/client/scanner-client.test.ts` were rewritten,
per JOB.md's root-cause diagnosis (`vi.useFakeTimers()` patches global
`setTimeout`, which Node's real `fetch` (undici) also relies on internally
for connection/keep-alive management — the retry's second real `fetch()`
against the local `node:http` mock server never resolved while the fake
clock was active):

1. `submitScan > retries once after a 429 with Retry-After and succeeds if the retry lands`
2. `pollScan > fails cleanly with ScanRateLimitError when the retry also gets a 429 (no infinite loop, no multi-attempt backoff)`

For both: removed `vi.useFakeTimers()` and `vi.advanceTimersByTimeAsync(...)`,
changed the mock server's `retry-after` header from `'2'`/`'1'` (seconds) to
`'0'`, and now simply `await` the real `submitScan`/`pollScan` promise
directly with real timers. `readRetryAfterMs` in `scanner-client.ts` does
`Number(header) * 1000` and only falls back to the 1000ms default when the
parsed value is `NaN` or negative, so `'0'` is treated as a valid, real,
non-negative delay (~0ms) — not the invalid-input fallback path. Added a
short comment on each test explaining why real timers are used here (so the
choice reads as deliberate, not an oversight, against the file's other two
fake-timer tests).

Behavioral assertions are unchanged:
- Test 1: `requestCount` reaches exactly 2 (1 failed 429 + 1 retry), and the
  retry's 202 is what `submitScan` resolves with (`result.status === 'queued'`).
- Test 2: `requestCount` reaches exactly 2 (1 original + 1 retry), and the
  second 429 causes `pollScan` to reject with `ScanRateLimitError` — the
  `expect(requestCount).toBe(2)` assertion still proves no loop / no
  multi-attempt backoff.

No other test, and no other file, was touched. `scanner-client.ts` was not
modified — this was purely a test-fixture timing issue, not a logic bug, as
stated in JOB.md.
