# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to keep this project practical, respectful, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying precise.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Disruptive or bad-faith behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate issues, discussions, pull requests, or other project
spaces to keep the project healthy.
